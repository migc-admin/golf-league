# Scorify Golf — Brand Guide
*For use in social media / content generation projects*

---

## 1. Brand Overview

**Name:** Scorify Golf
**Category:** Golf league management software (SaaS)
**One-liner:** The simplest way to run a golf event.

**Positioning:** Scorify Golf is modern, self-serve league software for independent golf leagues and social clubs — online registration, digital scorecards, live leaderboards, and side-game payouts, built for the way casual and semi-organized leagues actually play. It is not built for enterprise country clubs or single-round tournament ops; it's built for the weekly/seasonal league organizer who's doing this on top of a full-time job.

**Founder story (authentic angle — use for "why we built this" content):** Scorify was built by a golfer, for his own golf league — solving live scoring, leaderboards, and season-long standings out of personal need, not a top-down enterprise build. Founder-to-golfer authenticity, not vendor-to-buyer.

**First customer / proof point:** Mulligan's Island Golf Club, San Diego, CA.

**Target audience:** League commissioners and organizers at social/casual golf clubs — not just official club GMs, but informal league organizers running weekly or seasonal group play.

**Geography / tone context:** SoCal-based, year-round golf season — no seasonal urgency needed in messaging.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone

- **Plain and confident**, not corporate. Short sentences. No jargon.
- **Practical over aspirational** — lead with what it *does* (set up in minutes, score from any phone), not vague brand promises.
- **No hype language** ("revolutionary," "game-changing," etc.) — credibility comes from specificity (real features, real steps, real numbers like "16 players" or "$29/mo").
- **Golfer-to-golfer**, not vendor-to-customer. Founder is a golfer solving his own problem — content can speak from that authenticity.
- Emphasize **simplicity and speed**: "no app download," "no account needed," "set up in minutes."

**Sample taglines / hooks (from live product copy):**
- "The simplest way to run a golf event."
- "Set up in minutes. Score from any phone."
- "Everything your golf league needs."
- "From the first tee to season champion."
- "No app download required. Works on any browser, any device."

## 3. Visual Identity

### Logo
- Primary logo: `logo.png` (dark/full-color version, used on white backgrounds)
- Reversed logo: `WhiteLogo.jpg` (white version, used on dark/green backgrounds — e.g. footer)
- Logo is always paired with the wordmark "Scorify Golf" in bold, tight letter-spacing (`-0.02em`).

### Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Green** | `#1B4332` | Primary brand color — buttons, headings, icons, links |
| **Deep Green (footer/dark bg)** | `#01503a` | Dark backgrounds (footer) |
| **Gold Accent** | `#D4AF37` | Accent bar, badges, small highlight moments — used sparingly |
| **Ink (near-black text)** | `#1d1d1f` | Primary body/heading text on light backgrounds |
| **Muted Gray (secondary text)** | `#6b7280` | Subheadings, descriptions |
| **Light Gray (tertiary/footer text)** | `#86868b` / `#9ca3af` | Captions, footer links, fine print |
| **Off-white background** | `#fbfaf8` | Section background alternate to pure white |
| **White** | `#ffffff` | Primary background, text on green |
| **Border/hairline** | `#ebe9e4` | Card borders, dividers |

**Usage rule of thumb:** Green is the workhorse color (CTAs, icons, links, emphasis). Gold is a rare accent — used for a single highlighted word in a headline, a small accent bar, or a badge. Never use gold as a large fill or background.

### Typography
- **Headings:** `Manrope` (bold, tight tracking, `-0.02em` letter-spacing) — used for all major headlines and section titles.
- **Body / UI text:** System sans-serif stack (default Tailwind sans) — clean, no serif anywhere in the product UI.
- **Editorial/blog display font:** `Playfair Display` — used selectively for print assets/signage, not for core app UI or marketing headlines.

### Shape & Style Language
- Fully rounded pill-shaped buttons (`rounded-full`), bold sans-serif labels.
- Rounded cards (`rounded-2xl`) with hairline borders (`#ebe9e4`) rather than heavy drop shadows.
- Generous whitespace, centered max-width content columns (not edge-to-edge dense layouts).
- Icons: simple two-tone line icons (stroke width ~1.8), no filled/glyph icon style.

## 4. Product Pillars (for feature-highlight content)

1. **Online Registration** — shareable registration link; collects guest requests, notes, GHIN numbers automatically.
2. **Player Management** — roster import, handicap indexes, flights, groups in one place.
3. **Auto Group Scheduling** — one-click auto-assign by handicap, flight, or random.
4. **Printable Scorecards** — pro-quality exports with QR codes, tee times, course details pre-filled.
5. **Digital Scorecards** — scan a QR code or enter a group code, no app download or account needed.
6. **Live Leaderboards** — real-time updates hole-by-hole, visible from the fairway.
7. **Side Games & Payouts** — Skins, CTP, blind partners, and other buy-in games with automatic pot math.
8. **Season Standings** — points/standings tracked automatically across a full season.

**"How it works" 3-step narrative (good for carousel/reel content):**
1. **Set up your event** — add players or let them self-register; set format, flights, side games, entry fees.
2. **Share the link** — players tap a link, QR code, or invite code; find their name, start scoring. No download, no account.
3. **Play** — scores update live from every phone; leaderboard refreshes in real time.

## 6. Do / Don't

**Do:**
- Lead with concrete features and real numbers (16 players, $29/mo, 30-day trial).
- Use green as the dominant brand color; gold as a rare accent.
- Keep copy short, plain, and golfer-voiced.
- Reference the founder-built-this-for-his-own-league story when authenticity helps.

**Don't:**
- Don't use corporate SaaS hype language ("revolutionize," "disrupt," "game-changing").
- Don't position against big enterprise club/tournament platforms — Scorify's differentiator is independent leagues and social clubs, not enterprise facility management.
- Don't overuse gold — it's an accent, not a primary color.
- Don't imply an app download is required — "no app download, no account needed" is a core differentiator and should stay accurate in any messaging.
